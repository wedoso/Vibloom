Vocal separation on desktop and mobile uses Spleeter 2-stems by Deezer:
https://github.com/deezer/spleeter

The FP16 ONNX weights are downloaded on demand from the sherpa-onnx conversion:
https://huggingface.co/csukuangfj/sherpa-onnx-spleeter-2stems-fp16
